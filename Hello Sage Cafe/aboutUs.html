Our Story

Discover the journey of Hello Sage Cafe

Hello Sage Cafe is more than just a coffee shop—it's a story of passion, resilience, and community. Our journey began with a simple dream: to create a space where people could connect over great food and coffee. What started as a home baking business has evolved into a beloved local cafe, thanks to the support of our community and the unwavering dedication of our team.

Our founder, Drew Ramos, traded in her corporate career for a life of baking and creativity. From humble beginnings as "Desserts by Drewwwlll," she built a loyal following through her signature cookies and personalized cakes. But life had other plans when she became a mother to Sage. This unexpected turn of events didn't dim her passion—it ignited it in new ways.

Today, Hello Sage Cafe stands as a testament to following your dreams while being present for what matters most. Our team of skilled bakers, chefs, and baristas work together to create a warm, welcoming atmosphere where everyone feels at home. Whether you're a student studying late, a professional grabbing a quick coffee, or a family looking for a special treat, we're here to make your day a little brighter.

We've come a long way since our first location in Antrips Antipolo, and we're proud to have built a community that extends beyond our walls. Our "freedom wall" has become a symbol of expression and connection, where visitors share their thoughts, dreams, and stories. It's a reminder that we're all connected through our shared experiences and emotions.

As we celebrate our second anniversary, we remain committed to our original vision: creating a space where people can be themselves, express freely, and connect. We're constantly evolving, always striving to bring you the best coffee, pastries, and experiences. Thank you for being a part of our journey—every laugh, every sip, and every moment shared makes our dream possible.

-Drew Ramos, Founder and Owner
